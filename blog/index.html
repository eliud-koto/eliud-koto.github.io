Why I write these

Some of my best thinking starts as a question I can't put down. One question leads to another, and an hour later I'm somewhere I didn't expect. Increasingly that thinking happens in conversation with a language model — which, given what I work on, I find as interesting as the questions themselves.

The trouble is that those threads disappear. A few days later I remember I'd worked something out, but not how I got there.

So these are the ones worth keeping: questions I chased, what I found, and where I ended up. Some are about machine learning. Others are about science, education, or something I simply got curious about. I use models to explore, I check what they tell me, and I write the result.

I'm usually not writing because I've settled something. More often it's because I haven't.
