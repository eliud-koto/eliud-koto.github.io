An opening paragraph that states the question. Prose, not one-line fragments — a few sentences that set up what you were curious about and why it wouldn't leave you alone.

A second paragraph developing it. Links look like this, and emphasis is available where it earns its place: italic for a term being introduced, bold sparingly.

A sub-heading, if the post needs one

Sub-headings are useful once a post runs past a few hundred words. Short posts do not need them at all.

A quotation, or a claim worth setting apart from the rest of the argument so a reader can weigh it on its own.

Lists work when the structure is genuinely a list:

  • A first point.
  • A second point.
  • A third, if there is one.

A closing paragraph. It does not need to resolve the question — saying where you got to, and what you still don't know, is usually more honest and more interesting than a tidy conclusion.
